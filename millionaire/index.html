    How the numbers work

    • Returns compound monthly, at the rate that adds up to your yearly return after fees, so a 7% year adds exactly 7%. Fees come off as a share of your balance.
    • You invest at the end of every month. Your monthly amount goes up once a year by the raise you set. Amounts needed per month are rounded up to the next whole unit.
    • The projection assumes the same return every year. Real markets swing, so the odds and the shaded range come from 2,000 simulated market histories with the volatility you set, centred so that a typical history earns your expected return. The same histories are reused after every change, so differences come from your inputs, not from luck.
    • Inflation doesn't change your balance. It converts amounts into today's money and, if you choose, grows the target.
    • Taxes aren't included. They depend on your country and the kind of account.
    • This is a learning tool, not financial advice.
